# 5. The strip bay

Goal: use the strips as the student does: notes, order, coordination. About five minutes.

The strip bay has **the same strips** for the instructor and for the student, but **each has his own notes and his own order**:
what the student writes and moves, the instructor does not see.

## Step 1 — Two sections

- **Coordination**: a strip for every flight plan **waiting** in the Traffic List (and the IVAO aircraft near the field).
- **In contact**: the aircraft **flying**. When a plan starts, its strip moves here, with its notes.

Each strip shows the callsign, the ICAO type, the rules (**V** green, **I** blue), the entry and the altitude. The colour of
its edge is the kind of flight.

[![Two sections](gifs/t5-sections.gif)](gifs/t5-sections.gif)

## Step 2 — Notes

1. Click a strip to choose it.
2. Click a **quick note** (**DW, BASE, FINAL, OVH, BRK, SFO, ORBIT, T/G, LAND, L/A, L/P, G/A, TKOF, FRQ CNG**), or write
   a free note and press **Enter**.
3. Click a note to remove it.

[![Notes](gifs/t5-notes.gif)](gifs/t5-notes.gif)

## Step 3 — Order

**Drag a strip** to change its place: a coloured line shows where it will go. A strip moves only inside its own section.

[![Drag a strip](gifs/t5-drag.gif)](gifs/t5-drag.gif)

## Step 4 — Size and place

The strip bay floats like the other panels: drag its bar, resize it with the grip, close it with **▴** to a small box. On
the student's console the same.

## With IVAO

With **IVAO** on (manual, chapter 15) the aircraft of the network within 25 NM get a strip in **Coordination**, with the type,
rules and level of their IVAO flight plan. Drag it to **In contact** when it calls.
