# 4. Fly the traffic

Goal: give the orders — on the label of the aircraft, in AIRCRAFT CONTROL, with the keys. About fifteen minutes.

Start an aircraft in the circuit (Quick **LOCAL CONV**) and select it.

## Step 1 — The label

The label of the **selected** aircraft is the quickest way to give orders. It has rows: the title (callsign, class, leg),
**ALT** and **SPEED**, **PATTERN**, **SIDE**, **BRK**, **END**, then **DCT TO** and **NOW**.

- In each field the value **now** is yellow, the other choices are outlined. A **click** chooses one; the **mouse wheel**
  over the row goes to the next one.
- Click **ALT** or **SPEED**: a row of presets opens under it (**AUTO** gives back the circuit's own value).

[![The label](gifs/t4-label.gif)](gifs/t4-label.gif)

## Step 2 — AIRCRAFT CONTROL

The panel **✈ AIRCRAFT CONTROL** has the same orders in three groups:

1. **Altitude, speed and heading**: click, turn the wheel or type and press **Enter** (**Esc**: back).
2. **Next circuit**: pattern, side, break, end of the final — for the *next* touch and go or go-around.
3. **Now, in the circuit**: **DCT TO** (blue), **NOW** (orange) and the **urgent** ones (red).

[![AIRCRAFT CONTROL](gifs/t4-control.gif)](gifs/t4-control.gif)

## Step 3 — A heading

**Drag from the symbol** of the aircraft: a line follows the mouse; release it to give that heading. The aircraft **leaves its
circuit** and the label shows **MANUAL**. **Rejoin circuit** (key **R**) puts it back.

[![A heading](gifs/t4-heading.gif)](gifs/t4-heading.gif)

## Step 4 — DCT TO or NOW?

> **DCT TO** = **where** it goes: straight to that point of the circuit, then on with the circuit.
> **NOW** = **what it does now**, where it is.

Put an aircraft on the **downwind** and try both:

- **Turn in now** (key **N**): it turns base **from where it is**, after a short leg.
- **Base conv.** (key **F3**): it goes **straight to the start of the base** of the normal circuit.

[![DCT TO and NOW](gifs/t4-dct-now.gif)](gifs/t4-dct-now.gif)

The full explanation, with a drawing, is in the [manual](../MANUAL.md#9-dct-to-and-now-the-difference). A **grey** button
means the order makes no sense where the aircraft is.

## Step 5 — The urgent orders

On the final: **Go around** (key **G**). On the downwind: **Orbit** (key **O**; **Shift+O** on and on), **Change side**
(key **C**), **Breakout** (**Shift+U**).

[![Go around, orbit, change side](gifs/t4-urgent.gif)](gifs/t4-urgent.gif)

## Step 6 — The keys

Every button has its key (written on its right). **F12** shows the list in the program. Keys work on the selected aircraft
and not while you are writing in a box. **Tab** goes to the next aircraft.
