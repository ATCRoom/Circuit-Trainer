# 1. Your first lesson

Goal: an aerodrome set up, an aircraft in the circuit, seen by the student on his console. About ten minutes.

## Before you start

- You need a **map** of the aerodrome made with **ATCRoom Map Creator** (see the manual, chapter 1).
- You can work alone: the instructor's window and the student's console can be on the same PC (two windows).

## Step 1 — Open the map

Open **⚙ SETTINGS** (the small box at the top right of the map) → section **Map** → **Open map…** and choose the map of
your aerodrome.

[![Open the map](gifs/t1-open-map.gif)](gifs/t1-open-map.gif)

## Step 2 — Place the runway

Still in **Map**:

1. Write the **name** of the runway (for example `14`) and the **elevation** of its threshold in feet.
2. Press **Place on the map**, zoom in with the mouse wheel, click the **threshold** and then the **end** of the runway.
3. The opposite direction is made for you. Under **Runway in use** click the one you want to use.

[![Place the runway](gifs/t1-place-runway.gif)](gifs/t1-place-runway.gif)

Everything you set up is saved for this aerodrome: the next time the map is open you find it as you left it.

## Step 3 — Know the panels

Five panels float over the map: **SETTINGS**, **TRAFFIC GENERATION**, **TRAFFIC LIST**, **AIRCRAFT CONTROL** and
**STRIP BAY**.

- Drag a panel by its **bar** to move it; drag the **grip** in its bottom right corner to resize it.
- **▴** on the bar closes the panel to a small box in the top right corner; a click on the box opens it again.
- The place and size of each panel are remembered.

[![Move and close the panels](gifs/t1-panels.gif)](gifs/t1-panels.gif)

## Step 4 — Start an aircraft with Quick

Open **✎ TRAFFIC GENERATION** → **Quick** and press **LONG FIN**.

An aircraft appears on the long final, straight in, to a full stop. Click it (its symbol or its label): it is
**selected** (yellow) and its label grows.

[![Quick: long final](gifs/t1-quick.gif)](gifs/t1-quick.gif)

Try the others as well: **LOCAL CONV** and **LOCAL WIDE** take off and fly a circuit, **DEPARTURE** goes away along the
runway heading, **INBOUND IP** joins the overhead.

## Step 5 — Show it to the student

Press **STUDENT** on the bar. A second window opens: put it on the second monitor. It shows the traffic as a student
sees it (callsign, type, rules, altitude and speed, no commands) and his strip bay.

[![The student console](gifs/t1-student.gif)](gifs/t1-student.gif)

## Step 6 — Start the session (optional)

**START SESSION** starts a log of the exercise and puts the time back to 0:00:00. **PHOTO** (or **Ctrl+S**) saves a picture
of the map for the debriefing; **LOGS** opens the folder of the logs. See the manual, chapter 16.

## Step 7 — Finish

**PAUSE** stops the simulation, **RUN** goes on. To take every aircraft off the map, press **▼ WAITING LIST** in **TRAFFIC LIST** (their plans go back to WAITING).
